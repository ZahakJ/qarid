# قريض v2 — spec (extends design-ux.md and design-server.md; wins on conflict)

Clearer duel setup, an assist mode, a global search palette, accounts and
profiles, 1v1 rooms, and a CSS and motion pass.

## 1. Duel clarity (client)
- Every setup option carries a one-line Arabic explanation, visible rather than in a tooltip: what each tier changes («الخصم يجيب من مشاهير الأبيات فقط», …), الوصال vs المبارزة, and وضع السلسلة (روي vs الحرف الأخير حرفيًا) with a live example from a real بيت («كتابُهُ → الروي ب، والحرف الأخير ه»).
- A صدر alone is a valid answer, and the field says so: a helper line under the textarea («يكفي أن تكتب الصدر وحده — سنجد البيت») and a صدر-only placeholder.
- «كيف تلعب»: a five-step walkthrough of a real exchange, reachable from setup and the rules page; offered once on the first visit to `#/duel` (localStorage flag).

## 2. Assist / tutorial mode (server + client)
- `GET /api/game/assist?letter=<L>&q=<typed>&limit≤8`: أبيات whose first_letter = L and whose normalized text matches the typed prefix (FTS prefix query, last token starred), ranked by fame then bm25. Returns `BaitDto[]`, rate-limited with the game bucket.
- وضع التدريب toggle on setup (on for the first game, off after): from 2 typed characters a suggestion rail (debounced 250 ms) shows up to 5 matching أبيات; a tap fills the input. Assist scoring is a flat −50%, stated on screen. In normal mode there is no rail.
- A trailing `*` on any word is an FTS prefix in assist and in the palette. No regex. The palette footer documents it.

## 3. Global palette (client)
- Ctrl+K and Ctrl+F (and Cmd) open a palette on every route; Escape closes; `/` focuses it.
- Groups أبيات · قصائد · شعراء (5/4/4, poets fame-ranked), arrow keys mirrored, Enter opens, Ctrl+Enter opens `#/search` with the full list. Debounced 200 ms against `/api/search?scope=all`.
- The masthead's search entry («⌕ ابحث ⌃K») opens the palette. `#/search` stays for full results and deep links.

## 4. Accounts + profiles (server, writable db)
- `data/qarid-users.db`: a separate writable `DatabaseSync` in WAL mode; the corpus stays read-only. Tables: `users(id, username UNIQUE COLLATE NOCASE, display_name, pass_hash, created_at, last_seen)`, `sessions(token_hash PK, user_id, created_at, expires_at, ua)`, and the match tables of §5. Passwords: scrypt with per-user salt, compared with `timingSafeEqual`. Migrations by `PRAGMA user_version`.
- `/api/auth/`: `register {username, displayName, password≥8}` (open by default; with `REQUIRE_INVITE=1` it also takes `{invite}` checked against `INVITE_CODES`; 5/hour/IP), `login` (10/hour/IP), `logout`, `me`. Cookie `qarid_sess`: HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure when `PUBLIC_ORIGIN` is https, 90-day rolling. No email.
- `#/u/<username>`: display name, join date, duel stats, best streak, and the ترسانة snapshot if the user publishes it (`POST /api/profile/arsenal`, ≤8 KB). The owner can edit the display name. Solo play stays in localStorage; an account is for multiplayer and identity.
- Client: the settings store carries the auth slice; the masthead shows «دخول» or the account, a gold disc with the initial.

## 5. Multiplayer مساجلة rooms
- `rooms(code: 6 speakable letters, host_user_id, guest_user_id, starting_bait_id, mode rhyme|literal, timer_s null|30|60|90, status waiting|active|done, winner, created_at)`, `match_turns(room_id, turn_no, user_id, bait_id, played_at, ms_taken)`. Both players are signed in.
- Flow: the host creates a room from setup («ضد صديق»), picks the opening بيت (palette search or عشوائي, fame ≥ 2) and gets a share link `#/room/<code>`. The guest joins; the room goes active; the host's opening بيت is turn 0 and the guest answers first. Turns alternate, each through the same server-side verify as solo play, excluding every بيت and قصيدة already played. A wrong letter or an unknown بيت is a strike; 1–3 strikes (set at creation) or a timeout loses. The winner is recorded; a rematch swaps roles.
- Transport: WebSocket at `/ws/room/<code>` (`@hono/node-ws`), cookie-authenticated on upgrade; events join/state/turn/verdict/timeout/end/rematch. The server owns the clock. Every join replays full state; a dropped socket does not forfeit before the timer expires. `GET /api/room/<code>/state` is the polling fallback.
- Client: `#/room/<code>` reuses the duel components with a HUD for both players and whose turn it is. A signed-in visitor to a full room watches read-only. Recent matches and W/L appear on the profile.

## 6. CSS and motion
- Scrollbars themed app-wide: 10px, transparent track, gold-dim thumb with a hover glow, inner scroll areas included.
- One type scale for verse previews (Amiri, `--fs-bayt-sm`) and one for meta (`--font-ui`); no overflow at 1440, 1024 or 390.
- The duel's «المطلوب/عندك» wells are true circles (`aspect-ratio`, fixed inline size) with baseline-aligned Amiri letters and a concentric timer arc.
- شعراء: an Aref Ruqaa gold letter rail, poet cards that lift with a gold hairline and reveal the bio, an era strip with counts, staggered entrances, virtualized rows.
- Motion: staggered card entrances (60–80 ms, 8px + fade), route fades, chip and letter-grid micro-transitions, HUD count-up. All off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and no input waits on an animation.

## 7. Invariants
`shared/arabic.ts` is the only normalizer; the corpus is read-only and the users db is the only writable one; all UI is Arabic with Western digits; every list is paginated; every route is zod-validated and tested.

## 8. Design rules
- Spacing: one 4/8px scale, `--sp-1..--sp-9` = 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96. Component CSS may keep intrinsic sizes.
- Alignment: chip rows share baselines, card edges align across sections, logical properties only.
- Radii `--r-1`/`--r-2`; one shadow scale.
- Transitions: 150–250 ms micro, 300–420 ms entrances, one easing (`cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`); nothing bounces; text being read never moves.
- Motifs: ink, illumination gold, lapis, shamsa, corner pieces, dissolving rules.
